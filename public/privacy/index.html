Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

In short: you can play as a guest without giving us anything. If you make an account we keep your email, your username and your race results so you can get on the leaderboards. We don't show ads, we don't use tracking or analytics, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account from inside the game at any time.

Who we are

Zoomie Racers (zoomieracers.com) is a browser game run by Tom Briggs in the United Kingdom. He is the "controller" of your personal data under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018). For anything about your data, email support@zoomieracers.com.

What we collect

If you play as a guest, we don't create an account or keep any record of your races. The name you type for an online game is held only while that game is running. It is shown to the other players and isn't saved on our server.

If you create an account, we keep:

  • Your email address and username. Your username is public: it appears on the leaderboards and above your dog in races.
  • Your password, if you sign up with email. We only store a scrambled version (a salted scrypt hash) and can never see the password itself.
  • Your Google account ID and email, if you sign in with Google. Google also tells us your first name, which we use once to suggest a username and don't keep. We never see your Google password.
  • Your race results: the map, your dog, your finishing place, the number of racers, your race and lap times, Grand Prix results and the date. These make up the leaderboards and your own stats.
  • A sign-in session: a random code that keeps you signed in for up to 60 days.

From everyone, to keep the game working, our server writes short technical logs when a player connects or disconnects. They include the IP address, the time, why the connection ended, and a few details about the browser tab (for example how long it was in the background). We use these to fix connection problems and to stop abuse, such as too many sign-in attempts from one address. The logs overwrite themselves automatically after a short time.

Voice chat

Voice chat is off unless you turn it on. When it is on, your microphone goes straight from your browser to the other players' browsers in your game (peer to peer). It doesn't pass through our server and is never recorded. To connect, the browsers exchange network addresses, so the other players' browsers (and Google's public STUN address-lookup server) can see your IP address. Only use voice chat with people you're happy to talk to. You can mute yourself or turn it off at any time.

Why we use it (our lawful basis)

  • To provide the game you asked for (contract): your account, sign-in, races and leaderboards.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the game secure and working, such as connection logs and limits on sign-in attempts.

Who else sees it

  • Other players see your username, your dog and your results.
  • Our hosting provider runs the server that stores the data for us, under contract.
  • Google, only if you choose Google sign-in or turn on voice chat (see above).

We don't sell, rent or share your data with advertisers, or with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and race results until you delete your account. Deleting your account (Sign In → your name → Delete account) removes your email, username, sign-ins and every race result straight away. Connection logs are overwritten automatically after a short time.

Your rights

You have the right to see the data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to get a copy of it, and to object to how we use it. Most of this you can do in the game. For anything else, email support@zoomieracers.com and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

Anyone can play as a guest, but you must be 13 or over to create an account. If you think a child under 13 has made an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. If a change affects what we do with your data, we'll also say so in the game.
